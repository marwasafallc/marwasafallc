An LLC (Limited Liability Company) is a popular business structure in the USA. It can separate your personal assets from your business and is flexible to run. These services help you file the paperwork, get a registered agent and stay compliant.

What to look for

  • Check the filing fee and yearly fee for the state you choose, since they differ a lot.
  • A registered agent with a real US address is required in every state.
  • Ask what is included: EIN help, operating agreement, annual report reminders.
  • Compare the renewal price, not only the first-year price.

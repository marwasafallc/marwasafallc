Antivirus software and password managers protect your devices and accounts from malware, phishing and weak passwords. They are a basic need for anyone running a business online.

What to look for

  • Use a password manager and a different password for every account.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication wherever it is offered.
  • Keep your security software updated.
  • Do not install more than one full antivirus program at once.

A domain name is your website address, like yourbusiness.com. You register it yearly through a domain registrar and then point it to your hosting or website builder.

What to look for

  • Pick a short, easy-to-spell name, and prefer .com when possible.
  • Compare both the first-year and renewal prices.
  • Turn on domain privacy so your details are not public.
  • Enable auto-renew so you do not lose your domain.

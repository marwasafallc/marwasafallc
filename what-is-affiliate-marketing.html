What Is Affiliate Marketing? A Beginner's Guide

Blog › Affiliate Marketing
Affiliate Marketing · October 2026 · 5 min read

Affiliate marketing is a simple idea: you recommend a product or service, and you earn a commission when someone buys through your link.

How it works

A company (the merchant) gives you a special tracking link. When a visitor clicks your link and then buys, signs up or completes another action, the company can see that the visit came from you and pays you a commission. The visitor normally pays the same price as everyone else.

The main players

  • The merchant: the company that sells the product or service.
  • The affiliate: you, the person or website that recommends it.
  • The customer: the person who clicks and buys.
  • The network (sometimes): a platform that manages many affiliate programs in one place. Some companies run their own programs instead.

How affiliates get paid

  • Per sale: a percentage or a fixed amount for each purchase.
  • Recurring: a payment for as long as the customer keeps a subscription.
  • Per lead or sign-up: a payment when someone registers or starts a trial.

Always read each program's terms. Commission rates, payment dates and minimum payout amounts are different everywhere.

How to start the right way

  1. Choose a topic you can explain well. For example business setup, websites or online security.
  2. Build a useful website. Publish guides that really help people, not only a list of links.
  3. Join programs that fit your topic. Apply directly to companies or through affiliate networks.
  4. Place links where they help the reader. Recommend what fits the reader's need.
  5. Tell your readers clearly. Say that some links are affiliate links. Many countries and almost all programs require this.
  6. Track and improve. See which pages get clicks and make them better.

Common mistakes

  • Promoting products you know nothing about.
  • Hiding that a link is an affiliate link.
  • Writing fake reviews or making promises about results.
  • Spamming links in groups, comments or messages.
  • Breaking a program's rules, which can lead to a closed account and lost earnings.

Be realistic

Affiliate marketing is a long-term business, not a quick way to get rich. It usually takes months of regular, honest content before it earns anything, and there is no guaranteed income.

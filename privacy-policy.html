1. Introduction

Marwa Safa LLC ("Marwa Safa", "we", "our" or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to handling your information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, marwasafallc.com, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

This policy applies to our website and to any communication you have with us through it, including emails and messages sent through our contact form. By using this website, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the website.

2. Information You Provide to Us

We receive information when you choose to contact us by email or by using the form on our Contact page. This can include:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • the topic you select, such as a general question, business registration, website and hosting, AI and SEO, VPN and security, or a business enquiry;
  • the message you write and any other details you decide to include.

We use this information to read your message, reply to you, and keep a record of our conversation. You do not have to give us any personal information to read our guides and browse our resources. Please do not send passwords, payment card numbers, government ID numbers or other highly sensitive information by email or through the form.

3. Website and Technical Information

When you visit our website, basic technical information may be processed automatically by the services that deliver and protect the website. This can include:

  • your IP address and approximate location based on it;
  • browser type and version, device type and operating system;
  • the pages you view, the links you click and how long you stay;
  • the website you came from and the date and time of your visit.

We use this technical information to keep the website secure, to fix problems, to make sure pages load properly on different devices, and to understand in general how the website is used.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • to respond to your questions, requests and feedback;
  • to operate, maintain and improve the website and its content;
  • to decide which guides and topics are most useful to our visitors;
  • to protect the website and our visitors against spam, fraud, abuse and security threats;
  • to follow the law, resolve disputes and protect our legal rights when necessary.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not rent it to anyone.

5. Legal Bases for Using Your Information

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the law requires us to have a valid reason (a legal basis) for using your personal information. We rely on:

  • Your consent, for example when you send us a message and ask us to reply;
  • Our legitimate interests, such as keeping the website secure and improving our content, as long as these interests are not outweighed by your rights;
  • Legal obligations, when we must keep or share information to follow the law.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies work in the same way. This website may use them for:

  • Essential functions, so pages and features work properly;
  • Preferences, to remember simple choices you make;
  • Analytics, if we turn it on, to see which pages are popular and how visitors move around the site.

You can control cookies in your browser settings. You can block them, delete the ones already stored, or ask your browser to warn you before they are saved. If you block cookies, some parts of the website may not work as expected.

7. Analytics

We may use website analytics tools to learn general information such as how many people visit, which pages they read and which devices they use. We look at this information in summary form, to improve our content and design. We do not use it to identify you personally.

8. Email Communications

When you email us, we use your email address only to reply to you and to follow up on your request. We do not add you to a mailing list without asking first. If we ever offer a newsletter, you will be able to sign up on your own choice and unsubscribe at any time.

9. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We do not sell or trade your personal information. We may share it only in these limited cases:

  • Service providers. Trusted companies that help us run the website, such as website hosting, domain and email services, and the service that delivers contact form messages to us. They may process your information only to provide their services to us.
  • Legal reasons. If the law, a court or a government authority requires it, or when we need to protect the rights, safety and property of our business, our visitors or others.
  • Business changes. If our business is sold, merged or transferred, information may be passed to the new owner, who must continue to protect it under this policy.

10. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website contains links to other websites, products and services owned by other companies. We do not control these third parties, and we are not responsible for their content, security or privacy practices. When you click a link and leave our website, this policy no longer applies. Information you give to a third-party company is handled under that company's own privacy policy, so we encourage you to read it before you share any information or make a purchase.

11. How Long We Keep Information

We keep your messages and contact details only for as long as we need them to answer you, to keep reasonable business records, to resolve disputes, or to meet legal requirements. When we no longer need the information, we delete it or make it anonymous.

12. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect the information we receive, including using a secure HTTPS connection for this website and limiting who can access our email and website accounts. However, no method of sending or storing information over the internet is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you use the website and send us information at your own risk.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information. These can include the right to:

  • know what personal information we hold about you and receive a copy of it;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • ask us to limit or stop certain uses of your information;
  • take back your consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

Visitors in the EEA and the UK may also have the right to object to certain processing, to ask for their information in a portable format, and to complain to their local data protection authority.

California residents may have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, and not to be treated unfairly for using their rights. We do not sell personal information.

To make a request, email us at contact@marwasafallc.com. We may need to confirm your identity before we act on it, and we will reply within the time the law requires.

14. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" signal. Because there is no common standard for how websites should respond to it, we do not currently change how our website works when we receive one. You can still use your browser settings to control cookies, as described above.

15. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for adults and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it as soon as possible.

16. International Visitors and Data Transfers

Marwa Safa LLC is based in the United States, and our service providers may store and process information in the United States and in other countries. If you visit from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to, and handled in, countries whose data protection laws can be different from those in your own country. By using the website and contacting us, you understand and accept this.

17. Updates to This Policy

We may change this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when our website or the law changes. The newest version is always posted on this page with the date it was last updated. We encourage you to check this page from time to time. If you continue to use the website after a change, you accept the updated policy.

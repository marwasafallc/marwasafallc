Website builders let you create a site without coding, using templates and drag-and-drop tools. They are a good fit if you want to launch fast and manage everything in one place.

What to look for

  • Try the free trial or free plan before you pay.
  • Check that you can connect your own domain name.
  • Look at how easy it is to move your site later.
  • Choose a builder with the features you need, such as a blog or online store.

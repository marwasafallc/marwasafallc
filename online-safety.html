Services › VPN & Security › Online Safety

Good habits matter as much as software. Use secure email, strong passwords and regular checks to see whether your accounts were part of a data breach.

What to look for

  • Be careful with links and attachments in unexpected emails.
  • Check whether your email appeared in a breach, and change passwords if it did.
  • Use a separate email for your business accounts.
  • Never share your verification codes with anyone.

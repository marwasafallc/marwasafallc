Web hosting is the service that keeps your website online. Shared hosting is the cheapest and works well for new sites, while larger sites may need stronger plans.

What to look for

  • Check uptime promises, loading speed and support quality.
  • Look at the renewal price, which is often higher than the first price.
  • Make sure backups and a free SSL certificate are included.
  • Pick a plan you can upgrade as your site grows.

Services › Business Registration › UK LTD Registration

A UK private limited company (LTD) is registered with Companies House. You can do it yourself on the official government website, or use a formation agent that adds services like a registered office address.

What to look for

  • The official GOV.UK route is the lowest-cost way to register.
  • A UK company needs a registered office address in the UK.
  • Agents can help with address services, accounts and confirmation statements.
  • Read the requirements for company directors before you apply.

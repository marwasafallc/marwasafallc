How to Get Approved for Affiliate Programs

Blog › Affiliate Marketing
Affiliate Marketing · October 2026 · 5 min read

No one can promise that an affiliate application will be accepted, because every company has its own rules. But you can make your website and application look ready, and that helps.

What companies look at

When you apply, a reviewer usually opens your website and checks if it looks real, safe and useful to visitors. They also check that the information in your application is true.

Website checklist before you apply

  • Your own domain name (like marwasafallc.com), not a free subdomain.
  • A professional email on your domain, such as contact@yourdomain.com.
  • The key pages: About, Contact, Privacy Policy, Terms and an Affiliate Disclosure.
  • Real content: several helpful articles in your topic, written in your own words.
  • No empty pages: remove "coming soon" pages and placeholder text.
  • A secure, fast, mobile-friendly site with the padlock (HTTPS) in the browser.
  • A clear topic, so the reviewer understands who the site is for.

Application checklist

  • Give true information about yourself and your business. Names, country and payment details should match your documents.
  • Explain simply how you will promote the program, for example through guides and comparison pages.
  • Choose programs that fit your topic, instead of applying everywhere.
  • Read the program rules first, especially what is not allowed.

If you are rejected

  • Read the reason, if one is given.
  • Add more helpful content and improve your site.
  • Apply again after a few weeks, or try another program.

After you are approved

Follow the rules, use only the official links and banners, and disclose your affiliate relationship to your readers. Breaking the rules can end your account.

Approval is always decided by the company. A complete, honest and useful website gives you the best chance, but it is never a guarantee.

These tools keep your computer backed up, clean and secure. A good backup can save your business if a device is lost, stolen or damaged.

What to look for

  • Follow the 3-2-1 rule: three copies, two types of storage, one off-site.
  • Set backups to run automatically.
  • Keep your operating system and apps updated.
  • Test restoring a file from your backup once in a while.

How to Choose a VPN

Blog › VPN & Security
VPN & Security · October 2026 · 4 min read

A VPN can improve your privacy online, but not all VPNs are the same. Here is what to know before you choose one.

What does a VPN do?

A VPN encrypts the connection between your device and the internet, and shows the website the VPN server's IP address instead of yours. This makes it harder for others on the same network to see what you do.

When is a VPN useful?

  • On public Wi-Fi, like cafes, airports and hotels.
  • When you want more privacy from your internet provider.
  • When you travel and want access to your usual services, where this is allowed.

What a VPN does not do

A VPN does not make you anonymous or protect you from viruses and phishing. You still need strong passwords, updates and careful habits.

What to check

  • Privacy policy: look for a clear no-logs policy.
  • Speed and servers: servers in the countries you need.
  • Devices: how many devices one account covers.
  • Price: check the renewal price, not only the first deal.
  • Refund policy: so you can test it risk-free.

Check the law

VPN rules are different in different countries. Make sure that using a VPN is legal where you live, and follow the terms of the websites you use.
